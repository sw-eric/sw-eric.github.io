## Position

Long SKHY in two legs, both limit orders below the market. Leg one is 0.90 shares at \$155. Leg two is 0.46 shares at \$150, placed only if SKHY closes at or above \$148 on the day leg one fills. Full size is about \$210, or 15% of the account. SKHY closed the regular session at \$162.10 and traded at \$156.00 after hours. Thirty day realized volatility is 65%. I intend to hold three to twelve months.

## Why buy it back

I sold this stock fifteen days ago at \$149.89 and I am proposing to buy it back at roughly the same price. The bounce alone does not justify that. The post-mortem concluded that the stop closed the position, not the thesis, and since then the company has reported on the exact things the thesis depended on.

Third quarter revenue was ₩104.6 trillion, up 32% on the quarter, and operating profit was ₩83.3 trillion, an 80% margin against 76% in the quarter my August memo was built on ([press release](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/SKHY/sk-hynix-announces-3q26-financial-fcr2bh8ikc0x.html)). The margin matters less to me than the contract book. SK Hynix disclosed long-term agreements with fourteen customers covering more than half of its 2027 and 2028 capacity, typically on five-year terms. My original catalyst was the 2027 contract round, which I called the first real price discovery since the shortage began. That round has now partly happened, and the company came out of it with two years half sold.

HBM4 is expected to pass HBM3E in both volume and revenue this quarter, which answers the October 13 report about delayed back-end equipment. Samsung said on October 29 that it expects supply to stay tight through at least 2028 and that its 2027 HBM capacity is fully booked ([JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/samsungs-record-804b-q3-profit-fails-to-impress-investors/12944742)). The company is also returning about ₩100 trillion this year and holds ₩98.7 trillion of net cash.

The market is now selling AI suppliers alongside AI spenders. Alphabet fell 13% after guiding to roughly \$300 billion of capital expenditure in 2027, and Amazon is down 10.5% after hours tonight. Investors are punishing the companies writing the checks; SK Hynix is the one cashing them.

## Risks

The post-mortem's lesson was that a risk should say how it shows up in the price, so each one below does.

Korea is the risk that closed the first position. From October 5 to 9, SKHY fell 13.3% while Micron fell 3.3%, before any memory news. If SKHY trails Micron by more than ten points in a week with no company news, I am losing to Korean flows rather than to memory. I will not sell on that alone, but I will not add leg two into it.

Hyperscaler funding stress is the most likely way this fails in the next month. Negative free cash flow at Alphabet and Meta's \$37.5 billion of new common and convertible preferred stock ([Global Data Center Hub](https://www.globaldatacenterhub.com/p/meta-q3-2026-earnings-capex-cash-equity-raise)) could lead the market to price spending cuts before anyone announces one. That would look like SKHY falling on the same days as the spenders.

CXMT will probably show up again the way it did on October 12: a weekend headline and a one-day gap across every memory name. Unless it involves qualified HBM shipments, I treat it as noise.

I also read the contract terms more closely this time. LTA pricing moves within ranges agreed with each customer, so contracted HBM is protected on volume but only partly on price. I did not know that in August.

Finally, there is my own bias. I want the first trade back. The limits sit below Thursday's close so the market has to come to me.

## Entry

SKHY broke out of its \$130.79 to \$148.00 base on the print, closing at \$164.70 on October 28. The old resistance at \$146 to \$148 is now the first support, and both limits sit just above it. At a blended entry near \$153, the stop is 9.3% away and the \$190 target is 24% away, about 2.6 to 1.

## Limits

As set out in the [[investment-mandate]], the cap is 30%. I am sizing at 15% because of the volatility and because the last position stopped out. A 25% trailing stop goes in as a GTC order once each leg fills. The tighter stop governs: a daily close below \$139, under the October 21 and 26 lows. IBKR cannot stop on a close, so I will check after each session and sell at the next open.

The thesis is invalidated if 2027 HBM contracts are guided below 2026 levels, if HBM revenue declines for two consecutive quarters, if conventional DRAM contract prices turn negative quarter over quarter, if CXMT ships qualified HBM to a leading customer, or if any of Alphabet, Microsoft, Meta, or Amazon cuts its capital expenditure guidance.

One test is still open. On October 14 I said I wanted HBM revenue up quarter over quarter before re-entering, and the press release does not give that figure. I am entering without it on the strength of the HBM4 guidance, and I will check it against the call transcript.
