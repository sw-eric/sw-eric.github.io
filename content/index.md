Sonsu Research is derived from the Korean word *sonsu* (손수), meaning "with one's own hands."

I am Eric Lee, an undergraduate student at Columbia University studying Operations Research and Financial Engineering with minors in Artificial Intelligence and Psychology. I grew up across Korea, Malaysia, and Bangladesh, which gave me a real view of how capital moves between economies. Sonsu Research is an attempt to bring that lived context into a disciplined investment process and to build something of my own from scratch.

Sonsu Research is a personal research and trading project documenting my learning process while investing in the financial markets. The portfolio mainly operates in US and Asia-Pacific markets, reflecting my background and past exposures to these economies. The [[investment mandate]] and portfolio strategy can be summarized as a multi-strategy approach consisting of long-biased AI infrastructure and energy equities and options, as well as global macro plays concentrated on the US and various Asia-Pacific emerging markets. The project runs on personal capital that I have saved up through my time serving in the military. The main deliverable of Sonsu Research is the process, which includes written theses, post-mortems, and a documented record of decisions, not the return on capital, which is too small to be statistically meaningful.
